EXPLORE DRIFT

Ride a trail. Follow a clue. Hear a place differently.

DRIFT brings trail exploration and place-linked creative finds together in Hines Park.

A REALLY MINIMAL MAP

MINIMAL opens with trail lines on a quiet background. Switch to STANDARD for streets, or turn on signals, landmarks, and city labels under OVERLAYS.

HINES returns to the park. HINES PLACES stays visible for quick navigation. Tap a trail or marker for details; on a computer, you can also hover.

YOUR TRAIL COLLECTION

Browse Hines MTB routes, paved paths, and published access points in TRAILS. Save favorites on this device, show available routes on the map, or open access directions in Apple Maps.

Some routes have guide links rather than drawn lines. Use the MCMBA Hines guide and trail conditions when planning a ride.

WHAT IS A PSYCHOGEOCACHE?

In DRIFT, a psychogeocache is a creative find tied to a place: a sound, transmission, or clue that invites you to explore and notice your surroundings.

Psychogeography explores how places influence feelings and behavior. Curiosity, atmosphere, and unexpected details can guide a drift. Explore the idea at CU Boulder ↗

Geocaching contributes the search: follow locations and clues, find something, and record the discovery. Traditional caches usually include a physical container and logbook. Learn the basics at Geocaching 101 ↗

DRIFT combines them: a clue brings you somewhere, then the sounds, light, history, and mood become part of the encounter. The find can be a transmission rather than a box.

“Psychogeocache” describes DRIFT’s approach here. DRIFT is an independent project.

FIND FIELD SIGNALS

Enable SIGNALS under OVERLAYS, then tap a marker to open its clue and guidance. Choose written clues, a map reference, or celestial guidance when a precise location is available.

With location enabled, nearby signals become discoverable by proximity. Record an intercept when you reach one. The Signal glyph opens the Loudest Riot signal portal.

RECORD YOUR DRIFT

START DRIFT records a breadcrumb path using precise location fixes. STOP + SAVE keeps the walk in ARCHIVE, where you can reopen it on the map.

Saved walks, favorites, and personal intercepts stay on this device. Sharing an intercept to the public field log is optional.

LOCATION ON YOUR TERMS

Explore without granting location. Tap ◎ when you want it. Approximate fixes never recenter the map; recording and proximity discovery use precise fixes.

The app can retain its trail reference map for offline use after a successful online load. Street tiles and external links need a connection.
