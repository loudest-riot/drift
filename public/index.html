DRIFT // QUICK START

  1. 1. TURN ON LOCATION.Tap ◎ in the upper-right corner and allow location access.
  2. 2. PICK A DESTINATION.Stay on FIELD, then tap a signal card such as NEWBURGH POINTE or HELMS HAVEN.
  3. 3. FOLLOW A DIRECTION.On the signal page, start with CELESTIAL. Face the named star, then turn the number of degrees shown. Use FIELD MAP for the map or WRITTEN CLUE for words.
  4. 4. RECORD YOUR DRIFT.Back on FIELD, tap START DRIFT to draw your path. Tap STOP + SAVE when you finish.
  5. 5. REACH THE SIGNAL.Within 140 m, the transmission unlocks. Tap LOG INTERCEPT to save the discovery.
