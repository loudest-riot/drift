FIELD NOTES

What's changing as DRIFT takes shape.

New ways to explore, improvements from field testing, and a record of the project in motion. These are application updates, not live trail conditions. The currently deployed website may take a little time to reflect a source release.

  1. 09 OCT 2026RELEASE 25

    Hines Places, from end to end.

    A searchable catalog of named Hines Park places and landmark reference pins, with separate entries for Newburgh Pointe and Newburgh Lake Pointe Public Access. Unverified entrance pins are marked, not guessed. Browse the places →

  2. 09 OCT 2026RELEASE 24

    Photo Log: a visual field archive.

    A dedicated gallery gathers selected field photographs and images visitors explicitly share with verified finds. Open a photo to see its place, date, and caption. Private journals remain private. Open Photo Log →

  3. 09 OCT 2026RELEASE 23

    Field Notes gets its own place.

    A simple public update history joins DRIFT. Open it from ABOUT to see what changed without digging through technical commits.

  4. 08 OCT 2026RELEASE 22

    Waypoints and private observations.

    • Mark a cool spot, rock, geological feature, plant, bird sighting, or other point of interest.
    • Add dated observations to your private journal, without publishing them.
    • Map options, route recording, and other controls are tucked away more neatly on smaller screens.
  5. 08 OCT 2026RELEASE 21

    Bring a place into the map.

    Save a personal place or landmark with a pin and notes. You can submit a saved place for review; it will only appear publicly if approved.

  6. 08 OCT 2026PROJECT NOTE

    For Walter Wasacz (W2).

    DRIFT is dedicated to the memory of Walter Casimer Wasacz (1955–2026), whose life and art helped shape this way of exploring the world. Read the remembrance →

  7. EARLIER FIELD RELEASES

    Make it easier to enter the field.

    • A clearer first-visit guide and explanation of psychogeocaching.
    • Trail-first mapping, with optional road and discovery layers.
    • Improved location feedback and optional device-local profile and export.
    • NFC guidance explaining what a found card might open.
STILL IN MOTION

Field testing continues.

Interface refinements, waypoints, and finding the right balance between quiet exploration and useful guidance remain active areas of work. An idea mentioned here isn't a promise or a release date.
